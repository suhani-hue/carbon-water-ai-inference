# Research Question

## Primary question

Under what combinations of latency constraints, regional capacity, forecast error, workload intensity, and water-accounting assumptions does carbon- and water-aware routing of geo-distributed AI-inference requests improve—or worsen—the carbon–water–service-quality trade-off?

## Plain-English version

When does routing AI requests to cleaner or less water-intensive regions actually help, and when does it fail or make the environmental trade-off worse without breaking latency requirements?

## Study boundary

The study models operational electricity use and associated carbon and water impacts of inference serving. It excludes embodied carbon, embodied water, upstream supply-chain impacts, model training, and full life-cycle assessment.
